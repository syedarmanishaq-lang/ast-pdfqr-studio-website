Privacy Policy — AST PDF & QR Studio

Last updated: October 13, 2026

AST PDF & QR Studio ("the App", "the Website") is built on a simple principle: your files never leave your device.

Data we collect

We run no accounts, no sign-up, and no analytics of our own. Your files and tool inputs are processed locally and never sent to us. However, the Website and the App display ads served by Google, and Google's advertising services process limited technical data (such as cookie identifiers and your device's advertising ID) to serve and measure those ads. Details below.

Advertising — Website (Google AdSense)

The website shows ads served by Google AdSense (publisher ID ca-pub-3513714556256768). Google may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites, and to measure ad performance. You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ad Settings or at aboutads.info/choices. Learn more in Google's advertising technology policy.

Advertising — App (Google AdMob)

The Android app shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner on the home screen, a short video ad after a tool finishes, and optional rewarded ads (watch up to 4 per day to unlock 24 hours ad-free). AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID and share it with Google to serve and measure ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads. Apart from Google's ad serving, the app collects nothing — no account, no sign-up, no analytics.

How the tools work

All 16 tools (QR generator & scanner, PDF tools, image tools, converters, and utilities) run entirely in your browser / on your device using client-side code. Files you open with the tools are processed locally and are never uploaded anywhere.

Camera permission (app only)

The Android app requests camera access only when you tap "Scan" in the QR Scanner, and only to decode the QR code in view. Camera frames are processed on-device in real time; no photo or video is saved, stored, or transmitted.

Children

The App and Website contain no objectionable content and are suitable for everyone.

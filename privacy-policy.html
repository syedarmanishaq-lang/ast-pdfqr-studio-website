Privacy Policy — AST PDF & QR Studio

Last updated: October 13, 2026

AST PDF & QR Studio ("the App", "the Website") is built on a simple principle: your files never leave your device.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or user content. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising SDK, and no tracking of any kind.

How the tools work

All 16 tools (QR generator & scanner, PDF tools, image tools, converters, and utilities) run entirely in your browser / on your device using client-side code. Files you open with the tools are processed locally and are never uploaded anywhere.

Camera permission (app only)

The Android app requests camera access only when you tap "Scan" in the QR Scanner, and only to decode the QR code in view. Camera frames are processed on-device in real time; no photo or video is saved, stored, or transmitted.

Children

The App and Website contain no objectionable content and are suitable for everyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

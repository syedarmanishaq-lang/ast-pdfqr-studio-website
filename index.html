Why offline

Your files never leave your device.

Most online tools upload your documents to someone else's server. Ours can't — there is no server. Everything runs as JavaScript in your browser tab.

Private by architecture

PDFs, photos and passwords are processed locally. Close the tab and every trace is gone — nothing to leak, nothing to sell.

Fast, no queues

No uploads, no waiting rooms, no watermarks. A 200-page PDF extracts in seconds because it never travels the internet.

Works offline

Load any tool once and it keeps working with your connection switched off — on a plane, in a basement, anywhere.

FAQ

Common questions

Are these tools really free?

Yes — all 16 tools are free with no sign-up, no watermarks and no usage limits. They run on your device, so there is nothing for us to charge for.

Do my files get uploaded anywhere?

No. Every tool is 100% client-side JavaScript. Your files are read by your browser and never sent to any server.

Do the tools work offline?

Yes. Once a page has loaded, all its code and libraries are already in your browser, so it keeps working without an internet connection.

Which browsers are supported?

Any modern browser — Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari — on desktop or mobile. The QR scanner's camera mode needs a browser with camera access and a secure (HTTPS) page.

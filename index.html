Why offline

Your files never leave your device.

Most online tools upload your documents to someone else's server. Ours can't — there is no server. Everything runs as JavaScript in your browser tab.

Private by architecture

PDFs, photos and passwords are processed locally. Close the tab and every trace is gone — nothing to leak, nothing to sell.

Fast, no queues

No uploads, no waiting rooms, no watermarks. A 200-page PDF extracts in seconds because it never travels the internet.

Works offline

FAQ

Common questions

Are these tools really free?

Yes — all 21 tools are free with no sign-up, no watermarks and no usage limits. They run on your device, so there is nothing for us to charge for.

Do my files get uploaded anywhere?

No. Every tool is 100% client-side JavaScript. Your files are read by your browser and never sent to any server.

Do the tools work offline?

Yes. Once a page has loaded, all its code and libraries are already in your browser, so it keeps working without an internet connection.

What is the best free PDF splitter online?

AST PDF & QR Studio's PDF Split tool extracts any page range into a new PDF — free, no sign-up, and 100% offline so your documents stay private.

Can I convert text to speech free without an account?

Yes. Our Text to Speech reader uses your device's built-in voices — paste text, pick a voice, adjust speed, and listen. No account or limits.

Are PDF tools safe for sensitive documents?

Yes — safety is the whole point. Because every tool runs locally in your browser, sensitive contracts, IDs, and financial PDFs never travel over the network at all.

Which browsers are supported?

Any modern browser — Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari — on desktop or mobile. The QR scanner's camera mode needs a browser with camera access and a secure (HTTPS) page.

Can I use these tools on my phone?

Yes — every tool works in any modern mobile browser. The QR scanner can even use your phone's camera directly. No app install needed.

Do the tools work offline?

Once a page has loaded, yes. All processing happens locally with JavaScript, so airplane mode won't stop you after the first visit.

Is there really no premium version?

Really. All 21 tools are fully free with no accounts, no watermarks on free output, and no locked features. The project is sustained as a free utility, not a funnel.

How do I turn a PDF into a QR code?

Paste your PDF's link into our PDF to QR Code Generator — it draws a scannable QR code instantly, free, with no upload and no account. Anyone who scans it opens your PDF.

Is there a free alternative to paid PDF-to-QR services?

Yes — this studio. Paid platforms charge around $59 to host your PDF and sell you a dynamic code. Our PDF to QR tool is free forever: you host the PDF yourself (Google Drive or your site) and we generate a permanent QR code for its link. Nothing uploaded, nothing tracked.

Why 21 tools in one place?

Because your work does not live in one app. A typical week: you scan a QR menu on Monday, merge meeting PDFs on Tuesday, compress product photos on Wednesday, generate a password on Thursday, and convert a document on Friday. Each of those jobs used to mean a different website — most of them uploading your files to a stranger's server, stamping watermarks on free downloads, or paywalling the features that matter.

AST PDF & QR Studio takes the opposite approach. Every tool runs 100% in your browser using proven open-source libraries (pdf-lib, pdf.js, jsQR) — your files are processed locally on your own device and never touch our servers. There is nothing to upload, no account to create, no queue to wait in, and no "premium" tier locking the useful features. Twenty-one professional tools, one fast page, zero compromises.

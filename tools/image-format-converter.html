About this tool

Convert images between PNG, JPEG, and WebP in seconds. This free image format converter handles the most common real-world needs: turning PNG screenshots into smaller JPGs, converting legacy JPGs to modern WebP for faster websites, or exporting WebP back to universally-supported PNG.

Each format has strengths: PNG is lossless and supports transparency (best for logos and screenshots), JPG is tiny for photographs (no transparency), and WebP gives the best of both — smaller files with transparency support — though some older software still cannot open it. Conversion happens locally in your browser, so your images stay private.

Frequently asked questions

Which format should I choose?

PNG for logos, screenshots, and anything needing transparency. JPG for photographs where smallest size matters. WebP for websites — it is smaller than both with excellent quality.

Does converting lose quality?

PNG and WebP can be lossless. JPG always uses lossy compression — converting a JPG to PNG will not restore quality already lost.

What about transparency?

PNG and WebP preserve transparency. JPG does not support it — transparent areas become solid (usually white or black).

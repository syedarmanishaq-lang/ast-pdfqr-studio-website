About this tool

Add a professional text watermark to any PDF in seconds. Mark documents as DRAFT while they circulate for review, stamp CONFIDENTIAL on sensitive files, or brand reports with your company name — all without uploading anything to a server.

The watermark is drawn diagonally across the center of every page, in your chosen color and transparency, so it protects the document without hiding the content underneath. Because everything happens in your browser, even legal contracts and financial statements stay private.

Need to watermark a PDF online without uploading sensitive documents? This free PDF watermark tool adds custom text — DRAFT, CONFIDENTIAL, SAMPLE, or your own words — to every page of your PDF right in the browser. Pick diagonal or horizontal placement, adjust font size, color, and opacity, then download the stamped file. No sign-up, no watermarks on the watermark tool itself, and your files never leave your device.

Pro tips

Subtle beats loud. A 15–25% opacity diagonal 'DRAFT' or 'CONFIDENTIAL' protects without making the document unreadable. Heavy watermarks annoy legitimate readers.

Watermark before sharing, not after. Add 'DRAFT' to work-in-progress files before they leave your hands — once someone has the clean copy, you cannot take it back.

Use text watermarks for traceability. Watermarking each copy with the recipient's name or email discourages leaks far better than a generic stamp.

Frequently asked questions

Will the watermark be removable?

The watermark is drawn into the page content of a new PDF, so it cannot be toggled off in a reader. It can still be covered or cropped with PDF editing software, like any visible watermark.

Does watermarking work on scanned PDFs?

Yes. The watermark is drawn over the page regardless of its content — scanned images, text, or vector graphics all receive the same stamp.

Can I watermark only some pages?

This tool stamps every page of the document for consistent protection. Split the file first if you need different treatment per page.

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. Watermarking runs entirely in your browser with pdf-lib. Your document never leaves your device.

Will the watermark stop people copying my PDF?

It deters casual misuse and proves ownership, but a determined person can still screenshot. For sensitive documents, combine watermarks with limited distribution.

Can I remove a watermark later?

Add watermarks to a copy, and keep your clean original safe. Removing a baked-in watermark afterward is difficult by design.

What text should a watermark say?

DRAFT for work in progress, CONFIDENTIAL for sensitive files, SAMPLE for previews, or the recipient's name for traceable distribution.

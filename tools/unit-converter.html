Pro tips

Double-check critical conversions. Medicine dosages, engineering specs, and cooking for allergies deserve a second look. This tool is accurate, but verification is a habit worth keeping.

Know your system. The US still uses imperial (miles, pounds, Fahrenheit) while most of the world is metric. Converting for an American recipe or a European manual? Check which system the source uses first.

Temperature needs care. Unlike length or weight, temperature conversions aren't simple multiplication — the tool handles the offsets correctly, so trust it over mental math.

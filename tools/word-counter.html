Frequently asked questions

How is reading time calculated?

Using the widely-cited average of about 200–250 words per minute for silent reading. Speaking time uses roughly 130–150 words per minute.

What counts as a word?

Sequences of letters and numbers separated by whitespace or punctuation. Hyphenated compounds count per standard conventions.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No. Counting happens entirely in your browser's memory. Close the tab and your text is gone.

How is reading time calculated?

At an average adult reading speed of about 200–225 words per minute. Dense or technical text takes longer; light fiction goes faster — treat it as a solid estimate.

Does it count words in other languages?

Yes — the counter works with any language, including Urdu and Arabic script, counting words by whitespace separation.

Is there a character limit?

No. Paste anything from a tweet to a full manuscript — the counter handles it instantly, all locally in your browser.

About this tool

Scanned a document sideways? Received a PDF that opens upside down? This free PDF rotator fixes page orientation in one click — rotate the entire document or only the pages you select, by 90 degrees clockwise, counter-clockwise, or a full 180 flip.

Rotation is saved directly into the file, so the corrected pages open properly in every PDF reader, on every device. The page content itself is never re-rendered, which means text stays sharp and images keep their original quality.

Need to rotate PDF pages online without uploading your file? This free PDF rotate tool fixes sideways scans and upside-down documents right in your browser. Choose 90° clockwise, 90° counter-clockwise, or 180°, apply it to all pages or a custom range like 1, 3-5, and download the corrected PDF. No sign-up, no file size games, and your documents never leave your device.

How to use

  1. Add one PDF file.
  2. Pick the rotation and the pages (all, or a range like 1, 3-5).
  3. Click Rotate & Download.
  4. Save the corrected PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Does rotating change the PDF quality?

No. Rotation only changes the page orientation flag — the content itself is untouched, so text, images, and scans keep full quality.

Can I rotate only certain pages?

Yes. Enter a page range like 1, 3-5 to rotate just those pages, or leave it on all pages to rotate the whole document.

My scanned PDF opens sideways. Will this fix it permanently?

Yes. The rotation is saved into the downloaded PDF, so the pages open correctly in every reader — not just on your screen.

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. Rotation runs entirely in your browser with pdf-lib. Your document never leaves your device.

About this tool

Large PDFs get stuck in email outboxes and upload forms. This free PDF compressor re-renders every page at a smart resolution and re-encodes it as an optimized JPEG inside a fresh PDF — typically cutting scanned documents to a fraction of their size while keeping text perfectly readable on screen.

Three levels let you trade size against sharpness: Maximum for the tiniest file, Balanced for everyday sharing, and Light when quality matters most. Everything happens in your browser — no uploads, no queues, no watermarks.

How to use

  1. Drop in your PDF file.
  2. Choose a compression level.
  3. Press Compress and watch the live progress.
  4. Download the smaller PDF.

Frequently asked questions

How much smaller will my PDF get?

Scanned or image-heavy PDFs often shrink 60–90%. Text-based PDFs are already efficient, so gains are modest — the tool tells you the exact saving before you download.

Will the text stay readable?

Yes. Pages are re-rendered at a resolution chosen for screen reading and printing at normal sizes. Use Light compression for archival quality.

Is my document uploaded anywhere?

No. Compression runs entirely in your browser — the file never leaves your device.

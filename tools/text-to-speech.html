Text to Speech

Paste any text and hear it read aloud in a natural voice — perfect for proofreading, learning, and listening hands-free.

Not supported: your browser does not support speech synthesis. Try Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox.
Speech runs on your device with built-in voices. Your text is never uploaded or stored.

How it works

  1. Paste or type your text in the box.
  2. Pick a voice and adjust speed and pitch.
  3. Click Speak and listen.
  4. Pause or stop anytime — great for long articles.

Pro tips

Proofread by listening. Hearing your text read aloud catches typos, awkward phrasing, and missing words that your eyes skip over. Writers swear by it.

Slow down for learning. Reduce the speed when listening to a new language or complex material — comprehension beats velocity.

Try different voices. Voice quality varies hugely by device and browser. Cycle through the available voices to find one you enjoy for long listening sessions.

Frequently asked questions

Is this text to speech tool really free?

Yes, completely free with no limits, no account, and no watermarks. It uses the voices already built into your browser and device.

Can I change the voice and speed?

Yes. Pick any voice installed on your device, then adjust the speed (rate) and pitch sliders to taste. Slower speeds are great for language learning.

Does it work offline?

Most built-in device voices work offline once loaded. Nothing you type is ever uploaded — everything happens on your device.

What can I use text to speech for?

Proofreading your writing (hearing mistakes your eyes miss), learning pronunciation, accessibility, listening to articles hands-free, or just resting your eyes.

Is the text-to-speech really free?

Completely — it uses your device's built-in speech engine, so there are no accounts, no credits, and no limits on how much you listen.

Can I use it for another language?

If your device has voices installed for that language, yes — pick the matching voice and the reader handles pronunciation automatically.

Why do some voices sound robotic?

Voice quality depends on your device's speech engine. Modern phones include natural neural voices; older systems fall back to classic robotic ones. Try all available voices to find the best.

About this tool

This free QR code scanner reads QR codes two ways: live through your device camera, or from a photo or screenshot you upload. Point your camera at any QR code — a restaurant menu, a Wi-Fi login card, a payment code — and the decoded text appears instantly. Prefer not to use the camera? Drop an image file into the upload zone and the same local decoding engine extracts the content.

Privacy is the point: decoding happens entirely on your device with the jsQR library. Your camera feed is processed frame-by-frame inside the browser tab and is never recorded, transmitted, or stored anywhere. The scanner needs a secure (HTTPS) page and your permission before the camera activates, and you can revoke that permission at any time in your browser settings.

This free QR code scanner reads any QR code instantly using your device camera or an uploaded image — no app install and no sign-up required. Point your camera at the code or drop in a screenshot, and the decoded text or link appears immediately, all processed locally on your device. It is a handy online QR reader for checking links before you open them, reading Wi-Fi codes, and verifying printed codes.

How to use

  1. Allow camera access when prompted, or switch to the Upload tab.
  2. Point the camera at the QR code — or drop a QR image into the upload zone.
  3. The decoded text appears instantly below the preview.
  4. Use the copy button to copy the result to your clipboard.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my camera not working?

Camera access needs two things: a secure HTTPS connection and your explicit permission. Check that you tapped Allow when the browser asked, and make sure no other app is using the camera.

What kind of data can a QR code contain?

Any text: website URLs, Wi-Fi credentials, plain messages, contact cards (vCard), email addresses, and payment links. This scanner shows you the raw text so you can verify it before opening anything.

Is it safe to scan random QR codes?

Mostly, but stay alert: a QR code can hide a malicious link. This scanner shows the decoded text first instead of opening it automatically, so always read the URL before tapping it.

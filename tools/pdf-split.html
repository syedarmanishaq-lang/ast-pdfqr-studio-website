Pro tips

Use ranges to grab exactly what you need. Enter ranges like 1-5, 8, 11-13 to pull specific pages — perfect for extracting one chapter from a manual or one statement from a yearly file.

Split before sharing. Instead of emailing a 200-page document, split out the 5 pages someone actually needs. Smaller files, faster sending, less confusion.

Keep the original. Splitting creates new files; your source PDF is never modified. Work from a copy anyway — good habits prevent accidents.

Frequently asked questions

How do I split a PDF into separate pages?

Upload your PDF, type the pages you want — like 1-3 or 2, 5, 8 — and click Split & Download. You get a brand-new PDF containing only those pages, in the order you listed them.

Does splitting reduce PDF quality?

No. Pages are copied exactly as they are — text, images, and scans keep full quality. Splitting only selects which pages go into the new file.

Can I extract pages in a custom order?

Yes. Type pages in any order, like 5, 1-3, and the new PDF will contain page 5 first, then pages 1 through 3. Reorder freely.

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. Splitting runs entirely in your browser with pdf-lib. Your document never leaves your device.

How do I extract specific pages from a PDF?

Enter the pages you want as ranges (e.g. 1-5, 8, 11-13) and the tool builds a new PDF containing exactly those pages, in the order you listed them.

Can I rearrange pages, not just extract them?

Yes — list pages in any order (e.g. 5, 1-3, 9) and the new PDF follows your sequence. It is a quick way to reorder a document without desktop software.

Does splitting reduce quality?

No. Pages are copied without re-rendering, so text and images keep full original quality.

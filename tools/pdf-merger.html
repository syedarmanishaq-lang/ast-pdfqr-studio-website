About this tool

Combine several PDF files into one unified document. This free PDF merger is the fastest way to join contracts, append appendices, bundle invoices for the month, or assemble a report from separate chapters — without uploading sensitive files to a random website.

Add your PDFs in any order, drag to rearrange them, then merge with a single click. The tool concatenates pages in the exact sequence you choose and produces a clean, standard PDF. Because merging runs entirely in your browser, confidential documents — legal files, financial statements, medical records — never leave your device.

Need to merge PDF online without uploading sensitive documents? This free PDF merger combines multiple PDF files into one right in your browser. Drag files to reorder them, then merge everything with a single click — contracts, invoices, reports, or book chapters become one clean document. Because the merge happens locally with JavaScript, your files never leave your device, which makes this the safe choice for confidential paperwork.

Pro tips

Order matters — arrange before merging. Drag files into the exact final order first. Fixing page order after merging means doing the whole job twice.

Merge, then compress. Merged PDFs can get large. Run the result through an image compressor or our PDF tools if the file needs emailing — most inboxes cap attachments at 25 MB.

Your files stay private. Merging happens 100% in your browser. Contracts, IDs, and financial documents are never uploaded to any server.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a limit on the number of files?

No fixed limit — merge as many PDFs as you need. Very large combinations may take a little longer and use more memory.

Are bookmarks or form fields preserved?

Page content is preserved exactly. Interactive elements like form fields and bookmarks may not carry over, since the merger concatenates page content.

Can I merge only some pages?

The merger joins whole files in your chosen order. To pick specific pages, extract them first or merge everything and delete pages in a PDF editor.

Is there a limit on how many PDFs I can merge?

No hard limit — but very large merges (hundreds of pages or dozens of files) need enough browser memory. For giant jobs, merge in batches of 10–15 files.

Will merging change my PDFs' quality?

No. Pages are combined without re-rendering, so text stays sharp and selectable, and images keep their original quality.

Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

Only if you can open them — the browser needs to read the file to merge it. Remove the password first, merge, then re-protect the combined file if needed.

About this tool

Making QR codes one at a time is slow when you need fifty — for restaurant tables, product labels, event badges, or Wi-Fi cards. This free batch generator takes your whole list and draws every code in seconds, right in your browser.

Each code downloads as a crisp PNG with a sensible filename, and the ZIP option packs the entire batch into one file. No accounts, no per-code fees, no uploads.

How to use

  1. Paste your URLs or texts, one per line.
  2. Choose size and error correction.
  3. Press Generate.
  4. Download codes individually or as a ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

How many codes can I generate at once?

Up to 200 per batch. For larger runs, split your list and generate twice.

What should the error correction be?

Medium (M) suits most uses. Choose High or Maximum if codes will be printed small or might get scratched.

Is my list uploaded anywhere?

No. All codes are drawn locally in your browser.

About this tool

Text that is hard to read excludes people. This free contrast checker computes the exact WCAG contrast ratio between any two colors and tells you instantly whether the pair passes AA and AAA standards — for normal and large text.

Use it when picking brand colors, styling buttons, writing on photos, or auditing a website for accessibility. The live preview shows your exact pair at headline and body sizes.

How to use

  1. Pick a text color and a background color (picker or hex).
  2. Read the contrast ratio and pass/fail badges.
  3. Adjust until AA passes — AAA for critical text.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good contrast ratio?

4.5:1 or higher passes WCAG AA for normal text — the legal standard in most places. 7:1 passes AAA, the gold standard.

What counts as "large text"?

Text at least 18.66px bold or 24px regular. Large text needs only 3:1 for AA.

Does this work for color blindness?

Contrast ratio is one part of accessibility — it does not guarantee distinguishability for every type of color vision. Avoid conveying meaning by color alone.

About this tool

A plain black-and-white QR code works, but a branded one gets scanned more. This free tool puts your logo in the center of the QR code — perfect for restaurant menus, product packaging, business cards, event posters, and Wi-Fi signs that carry your brand.

Scannability is protected by design: the generator uses the highest error-correction level (H), so the code survives the logo covering its middle, and your logo sits on a clean white badge that keeps the contrast crisp for phone cameras.

Want a QR code with a logo online without signing up anywhere? Upload your logo, type your link or text, and download a print-ready PNG — all free, all in your browser. The high error-correction level means the code scans reliably even with your emblem in the center, making it ideal for menus, packaging, and marketing material.

How to use

  1. Type the text or URL the code should open.
  2. Upload your logo image.
  3. Pick a size (512 px for screens, 1024 px for print).
  4. Download the PNG.

Frequently asked questions

Will a QR code with a logo still scan?

Yes. This tool uses the highest error-correction level (H), which lets a QR code lose up to 30% of its area and still scan. The logo covers far less than that, on a clean white pad.

What logo image should I use?

A square PNG with a transparent or white background works best. The logo is scaled to fit a centered badge, so wide or tall images are letterboxed automatically.

Can I use this QR code commercially?

Yes — codes you generate here are yours to use anywhere: menus, packaging, business cards, posters, or your website. No attribution required.

Is anything uploaded?

No. The QR code and logo compositing happen entirely in your browser. Your logo image never leaves your device.

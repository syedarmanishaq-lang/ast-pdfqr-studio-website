About this tool

Upload forms, social networks, and marketplaces all demand specific image sizes. This free image resizer changes any photo's dimensions in seconds — pick a preset for common jobs or type exact pixels. The aspect-ratio lock prevents the stretched-photo look, and you choose PNG, JPEG, or WebP output.

Resizing happens on an HTML canvas in your browser with high-quality smoothing, so edges stay crisp. Nothing is uploaded — your photos stay on your device.

How to use

  1. Drop in your image.
  2. Pick a preset or enter width/height in pixels.
  3. Choose the output format.
  4. Press Resize and download.

Frequently asked questions

Will resizing reduce quality?

Downscaling is safe — the tool uses high-quality smoothing. Upscaling beyond the original size can look soft, so the tool warns you via the dimension readout.

Which format should I choose?

PNG for graphics and transparency, JPEG for photos you want small, WebP for the best size-to-quality ratio on modern sites.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Resizing runs entirely in your browser.
